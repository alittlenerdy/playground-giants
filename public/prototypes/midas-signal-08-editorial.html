NLE Choppa’s “Shotta Flow” came out in 2019. Midas800 produced it. The record reached #36 on the Billboard Hot 100 and is certified 2x Platinum by the RIAA.

Genius sat down with him for its Deconstructed series and took the record apart piece by piece. That film is above. It has been watched 1,035,627 times.

The beats themselves live on his YouTube channel, Midas800: 141 videos, 14,600 subscribers, playlists called “Best of 800” and “FL Tutorials.” Some of the beats carry the name. One, “Shotta Flow 8,” went up with the caption If I dropped w/ NLE Choppa again.

Seven of them are below, with his drum kit. Pick one, name a price, and it goes to your inbox.
