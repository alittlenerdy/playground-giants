What is registered, and what is not

"I don't even know. I definitely lost track."

That sentence is why this page exists. Every red mark above is a lane where money either is or is not arriving, and right now nobody can say which. Not a single one of them is a guess about whether the work is good. They are all questions about whether the paperwork followed the record.

The first job is to turn every red mark green or prove it was already fine. Both are real answers, and both are worth having in writing.
