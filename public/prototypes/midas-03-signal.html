How this actually works — read this part

Nobody can show you who is streaming your music. Spotify and Apple return aggregate figures only: top cities, countries, age bands. No names, no contact details, no way to send a message. That is a platform rule, not a technical limit, and no product gets around it.

What this map shows is everyone who opted in through something you own — a free download, a licence, a kit purchase, a stream sign-up. That list starts smaller than your stream count and it grows every week, and unlike a follower count it belongs to you.

For a producer this works better than it does for an artist. A rapper's listeners are anonymous streams. Your listeners are buyers, and buyers hand over an email to complete a transaction. You can capture very nearly all of the people who matter commercially.
