How this works · read this part

Payments are not connected. This is a prototype. Nothing is charged, nothing is emailed, and the download link is a mock.

You pick the price. The amounts under the price box are examples. Whether there is a minimum is Midas's call and has not been decided. License terms: to be set with Midas.

Every download is a pin, and the list is his. Whatever you pay, getting a file adds your email, with your OK, to Midas's own list rather than a platform's. Each one shows up on the map at city level. The pins on this page are samples. Real ones appear as people download.

No photos yet. There are no recent photos and the shoot isn't booked, so the page is built from type and color. The one photo slot says what it's waiting for.
