How this works · read this part

The map is the point. Every download, whatever you pay, adds your email, with your OK, to Midas's own list. Each person on that list is one pin, placed by city. It belongs to him, not to a platform, and nobody can switch it off.

The pins you see are samples. Real ones appear as people download. Tooltips show city names only. There are no counts on this page because a prototype has no real downloads to count.

Payments are not connected. This is a prototype: nothing is charged and nothing is emailed. The price is yours to name. Whether there is a minimum is Midas's call and not decided. License terms: to be set with Midas.

No photos yet. The shoot isn't booked, so the page runs on type and color. The one photo slot says what it's waiting for.
