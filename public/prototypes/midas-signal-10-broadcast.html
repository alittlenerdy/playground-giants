    How this works, read this part

    Streams are anonymous. Downloads are not. YouTube and the streaming services show a producer totals, never who listened, and there is no way to reach those people. An email handed over for a download is different: it belongs to Midas800, not to a platform.

    So every download here, at any price, adds an opted-in email to his own list. That list is what the map is for. Each pin will be a real person who chose to download. The pins on it today are samples.

    What is not decided yet: the minimum price is Midas's call, the chip amounts are examples, and license terms are to be set with Midas. Nothing on this page grants or describes rights. There are no photos yet, and the camera slot says what the shoot needs.
