    How this works, read this part

    Streams are anonymous. Downloads are not. Platforms show a producer totals, never who listened. A download that goes to your email is different: Midas800 can actually reach you.

    Every download here, at any price, adds an opted-in email to his own list. Not a platform's list, his. That is what the map will show: one pin per real person. Today's pins are samples.

    Not decided yet: the minimum price is Midas's call, the amounts on the chips are examples, and license terms are to be set with Midas. There are no photos on this page because the shoot has not happened yet.
