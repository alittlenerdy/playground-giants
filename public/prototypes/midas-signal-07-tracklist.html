    How this page works. Read this part.

    Payments are not connected. The checkout runs in your browser only. Nothing is charged, there is no file behind the download link, and no email is sent.

    Two decisions are still open. The minimum price is Midas's call and has not been decided, including whether $0 is allowed. License terms will be set with Midas. The amounts on the chips are examples only.

    The list is the point. Every download adds an opted-in email to Midas's own list. Streaming services do not tell a producer who is listening; a download that asks for an email does. That list is what fills the map, which for now shows sample pins only.

    What is real here. Beat names, YouTube links and lengths come from the Midas800 channel. The waveforms are drawn for illustration and do not come from the audio. No photos are used, because none exist yet; a shoot is needed.
