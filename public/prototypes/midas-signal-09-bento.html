      How this works. Read this part.

      Nothing is charged. Payments are not connected. The checkout runs in your browser only, the download link is a mock and no email is sent.

      Two decisions are Midas's. The minimum price is not decided, including whether $0 is allowed. License terms will be set with Midas. The chip amounts are examples.

      The list is the point. Every download adds an opted-in email to Midas's own list. Streaming services do not tell a producer who is listening; a download that asks for an email does. The map fills from that list. Its pins are samples for now.
