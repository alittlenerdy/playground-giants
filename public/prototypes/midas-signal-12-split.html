How this works, read this part

Streams are anonymous. Downloads are not. Platforms show a producer totals, never who listened. An email given for a download is a direct line, and it belongs to Midas800 rather than to a platform.

That is why every download here, at any price, adds an opted-in email to his own list. The map is that list drawn out. The pins you see now are samples; real ones replace them as people download.

Still to decide: the minimum price is Midas's call, the chip amounts are examples, and license terms are to be set with Midas. There are no photos yet; the photo slot shows what the shoot needs to cover.
